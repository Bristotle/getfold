## 1. Purpose

Fold has no customers yet. The purpose of this engagement is to find out, from real church secretaries, treasurers, pastors and administrators, what frustrates them about how they keep their records today, in their own words, and whether Fold answers it. Months one and two are a market test. Month three scales what the test proved.

The Consultant's own framing, which both parties adopt: this is discovery before promotion. The primary output is evidence, not reach.

## 2. Term

## 3. Services and deliverables

### Month 1: Real Customer Discovery

**Checklist for payment of Month 2:**

- Target denominations confirmed with the Client. The starting list is Methodist, Presbyterian, Pentecostal, Anglican, AME Zion and Baptist.
- Two to three churches confirmed as willing to give honest early feedback.
- First discovery content and direct outreach live within the first week.
- **At least eight structured conversations** with church decision makers or influencers (secretaries, treasurers, pastors, presiding elders, class leaders, administrators), which is the two conversations a week the Consultant committed to. Each conversation documented in the person's own words: who they are, what church, what frustrates them, what they use today, what they would need to see before trusting a new system.
- A light social presence maintained so the brand exists when somebody searches: **four to six posts** in the month across the agreed platforms, rather than the twelve in the proposal. The difference in effort goes to conversations.

### Month 2: Validate and Refine

**Checklist for payment of Month 3:**

- Real pain points documented from Month 1 conversations, consolidated and grouped.
- A **draft messaging brief** written from those conversations: what to say, to whom, in what words, and what objections to expect.
- At least eight further structured conversations, documented as above.
- Two or three messaging angles tested in small pushes, with what was learned recorded.
- **Two to three churches invited onto the thirty day free trial**, with the Consultant's support through setup where the church wants it.
- Four to six posts.

### Month 3: Increase

**Checklist for the final review:**

- The **validated messaging brief finalised** and delivered to the Client as a written document, including verbatim quotes with the speaker's permission to be quoted.
- Content built from tested language, with posting frequency increased as agreed at the Month 2 review.
- Trial invitations pushed with confidence, toward sign ups.
- **A written final report**: what was learned, what resonated, what did not, which churches are on the trial, and a clear recommendation for month four and beyond.
- Best efforts to secure **at least one church willing to be named and quoted** publicly. The parties recognise this depends on the church's consent and is a target rather than a guarantee.

### Platforms

Facebook, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, with WhatsApp used for follow up with contacts gathered through the other channels and through direct outreach. Instagram may be used at the Consultant's discretion but is not a required platform. Any change is agreed at a monthly review.

## 8. Confidentiality

The Consultant will see things that are not public: which churches are in conversation, what they said, Fold's pricing and product plans, and the two audits the Client has shared. She will keep them confidential during the engagement and for two years afterwards, and will not use them for any other client. The Client will keep the Consultant's rates and methods confidential on the same terms.

## 9. Personal data

The conversations at the heart of this engagement produce personal data: names, roles, phone numbers and opinions of people at churches, and the church a person belongs to is itself an indicator of religious belief, which is special personal data under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

The Consultant will therefore:

- Tell each person, at the start of a conversation, that she is gathering feedback on behalf of Fold and how it will be used.
- Obtain a person's clear agreement before quoting them by name or by church in any material, and record that agreement.
- Not add anyone to a mailing list, group or broadcast without their consent.
- Keep the data only for this engagement, hand it to the Client on completion, and delete her own copies on request.

The Client is the data controller for this data and will register with the Data Protection Commission as required.

The Consultant will disclose any existing relationship with any of these before signing.
